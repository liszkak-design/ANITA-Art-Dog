Baza: `templates/urny_propozycja_tekstow.md` (zatwierdzona 28.10.2026: nagłówki zestaw 1, tytuły, tagi, banki, sekcje stałe).
Koty i konie nie mają wpisów NICK/SYN w kolumnie E (jak w płytkach), więc używamy wyłącznie banków A1–A5. Wiele ras koni ma w nazwie Horse lub Pony,
dlatego `{BREED}` nie jest łączone z rzeczownikiem "horse" (unikamy "Arabian Horse horse"). Dla koni i kucyków nie piszemy "ashes of your horse" (patrz pytanie na dole).

Pozostałe 5 stałych i pula bez zmian; przy kotach w puli bez tagów "dog", przy psach i koniach bez "cat".

## Sekcje stałe: kot (dla konia i kucyka zmiany niżej)

```
- Handmade wooden urn for pet ashes, made of solid wood and plywood, painted brown
- Oval ceramic plaque with a printed sketch-style portrait of a {BREED}, approx. 7.5 x 10.5 cm (2.95 x 4.13 in), permanently attached
- Paw-shaped plaque with an engraved quote, and a nameplate with the name and dates
- External size: 21 x 29 x 22 cm (8.3 x 11.4 x 8.7 in)
- Internal size: 14 x 21 x 15.5 cm (5.5 x 8.3 x 6.1 in), capacity approx. 4.5 litres (275 cubic inches)

- Removable lid, not sealed: glue it with any all-purpose glue to close it tightly

Losing a cat is one of the saddest days. This urn is a quiet, lasting place to keep your cat close. The brass handles and carved decorations give it a classic, timeless look, and the engravings are applied to painted plywood, so every urn is made for one cat only.

PERSONALISATION
Every urn is made to order. Please tell us:
- Name: the name of your pet, as it should appear on the nameplate.
- Dates: dates of birth and death, in any format you like.
- Quote: the paw-shaped plaque carries "You left paw prints on my heart" unless you give us your own exact wording.
- Plaque photo: the plaque shows the sketch-style portrait you see, or we print your own photo.
- Print style: original photo, full colour with the background removed, or pencil sketch.

Questions about personalising your urn? Send us a message, we will gladly help.

We would be honoured to help you say goodbye to your beloved cat with dignity.

INTERNATIONAL ORDERS
(bez zmian, jak w płytkach)
```

## Pytania personalizacyjne Etsy (5, jak w ofercie 660269548 z poprawkami zaakceptowanymi 28.10.2026)

1. Name (tekst, wymagane, 50 zn.)
2. Dates (tekst, wymagane, 50 zn.)
3. Quote on the plaque (optional), tekst, 600 zn. Instrukcja (psy/koty): "Leave empty for the default quote, or type the exact text you'd like engraved on the paw-shaped plaque." Konie: "...on the horseshoe-shaped plaque."
4. Pet photo (wgranie 1 pliku). Instrukcja: "Please upload a high-resolution image. The better the photo quality, the better the print will look on the plaque."
5. How should we print the photo? (lista): Color + background / Color, no background / Pencil sketch.

Uwaga: etykieta opcji ma limit 20 znaków Etsy, dlatego nie ma "Color, with background" (22 zn.), zostaje obecne "Color + background" (18).
